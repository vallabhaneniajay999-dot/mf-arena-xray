Delete your account

You can delete your MF Arena account and all its data at any time, from inside the app.

Effective 28 October 2026

How to delete

  • Open the app and sign in.
  • Go to Profile → Privacy & data.
  • Tap “Delete my account and data” and confirm.

What is deleted

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Private database backups that still contain your data are removed as they age out (at most about two months).

  • Your portfolios, folios, transactions, holdings and all calculated results.
  • Any stored statement PDFs, your PAN and statement password.
  • Your Gmail connection — we also revoke the access at Google.
  • Your screen-open counts, Pro waitlist choice and plan record.
  • Your sign-in account.

Can’t open the app?

Email mfarena.importer@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, and we will delete the account for you.
