See inside your mutual funds

MF Arena reads your own CAMS or KFintech statement and shows the facts about your money in one place. Facts about your portfolio, never advice.

  • Your real returnValue, what you put in, gain and XIRR, updated with the latest NAV every day.
  • What your funds actually holdThe stocks inside all your funds added together, and which funds hold the same stocks.
  • What it costs youYearly fees, Direct vs Regular, and the tax if you sold today.
