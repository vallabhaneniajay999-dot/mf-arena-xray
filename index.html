Questions

Which statements work?

The Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) from CAMS or KFintech, which covers mutual funds from every fund house. Connect Gmail (read-only) and we find it for you, or forward it to your private import address.

Do you need my CAMS, broker or bank password?

No. We never ask for your mutual fund, broker or bank passwords. You sign in with a code sent to your email.

What happens to my statement?

The statement file is deleted after it is read. Your PAN and statement passwords are stored encrypted, and you can download or delete everything from inside the app.

Can MF Arena buy or sell funds?

No. It only reads your statement and shows the numbers. It cannot place orders or move money.

Is this investment advice?

No. MF Arena shows facts and calculations about your own portfolio. It does not recommend any fund.

Is MF Arena free?

Your value, returns, funds and transactions are free. Some detail screens will be part of MF Arena Pro, which is not on sale yet: early testers get the first year free when it opens.
