What the app is

MF Arena shows information and analytics about mutual fund holdings that you import. It does not buy, sell or hold any investment, and it is not a broker, distributor or investment adviser.

Not investment advice

Nothing in the app is a recommendation to buy, sell, switch or hold any security or fund. Figures describe your own portfolio. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks; read all scheme-related documents carefully. Please consult a SEBI-registered investment adviser before making investment decisions.

Accuracy of data

  • Your figures come from the statements you import, NAVs published by AMFI, and portfolio disclosures published by fund houses. These can be delayed, revised or incomplete.
  • Fund holdings (X-Ray) are as disclosed by the fund house, usually monthly — not real-time.
  • Where data is missing we show it as unavailable rather than guessing. Always check important figures against your official statement.

Your account

  • Import only statements that belong to you, or that you are authorised to use.
  • Keep access to your email account secure — it is how you sign in.
  • Do not misuse the app: no attempts to access other users’ data, overload the service, or reverse-engineer it.
